# AstroSage Call Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes AstroSage customer interaction data to identify
demand patterns, operational gaps, revenue opportunities, agent
performance issues, and customer satisfaction trends.

The analysis covers 28,027 customer interactions across 30 attributes,
including calls, chats, Gurus, ratings, revenue, and interaction status.

The project combines Excel-based data analysis with an interactive
dashboard and data storytelling presentation to convert raw interaction
data into actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The key objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze call and chat volumes
- Identify peak days and hours
- Evaluate call and chat completion
- Analyze agent workload and performance
- Measure customer satisfaction
- Identify operational and technology gaps
- Recommend an effective allocation of the ₹1 Crore investment

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains:

- 28,027 customer interaction records
- 30 attributes
- Customer calls and chats
- Consultation types
- Guru/agent information
- Interaction status
- Revenue information
- Customer ratings

The data was cleaned and prepared in Excel before analysis.

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following activities were performed during data preparation:

- Standardized date and time fields
- Standardized categorical and numerical values
- Removed duplicates
- Used TRIM and PROPER for text standardization
- Handled blank values using IF and ISBLANK
- Created derived fields such as Date, Month and Hour
- Used Paste as Values where required

## 📈 Key Findings

### Customer Demand

- Average daily call volume was approximately 245.97 calls.
- December recorded the highest call volume with 8,090 calls.
- January recorded the lowest call volume with 418 calls.
- Demand varied significantly across time periods.

**Business implication:** Flexible, demand-based workforce planning
is more effective than maintaining fixed staffing levels.

---

### 💰 Revenue & Interaction Performance

- Calls were the largest revenue-generating consultation type.
- Calls generated approximately ₹1.69 lakh in the analyzed data.
- The call channel was strategically important for revenue protection
  and improvement.

**Business implication:** Improving call-service reliability and
performance should be a key business priority.

---

### 👥 Agent Performance & Customer Satisfaction

- Average calls handled per agent per day: 1.88
- 1,275 repeat callers were identified.
- Repeat callers represented 35.19% in the submitted analysis.
- Correlation between call duration and customer satisfaction was
  approximately 0.0548.
- Highest Guru satisfaction score: 7.5
- Lowest Guru satisfaction score: 0
- December's average rating: 2.94
- January's average rating: 2.67

**Business implication:** Increasing call duration alone is unlikely
to improve customer satisfaction. Service quality, successful
resolution and overall customer experience are more important.

---

## 💰 ₹1 Crore Investment Recommendation

Based on the analysis, the recommended investment allocation was:

| Area | Investment | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Training | ₹50 lakh | 50% |
| Technology | ₹30 lakh | 30% |
| Hiring | ₹20 lakh | 20% |
| **Total** | **₹1 Crore** | **100%** |

The analysis indicates that the primary challenge is not simply a
shortage of agents. Low customer satisfaction and service-quality
gaps indicate that improving agent skills and customer handling
should be a major priority.

---

## 🛠️ Investment Risk & Mitigation

### Technology
**Risk:** High implementation cost and possible implementation failure.

**Mitigation:** Pilot new technologies and implement them gradually.

### Training
**Risk:** Training may not translate into improved performance.

**Mitigation:** Measure customer ratings, completion rates and agent KPIs.

### Hiring
**Risk:** Higher fixed costs without solving the underlying service problems.

**Mitigation:** Hire only where workload and demand data supports the need.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Improve Service Efficiency

Invest in technology such as smart call routing, IVR, chatbots and
agent-assist tools to improve successful interactions and reduce
waiting time.

### 2. Improve Agent Performance

Implement targeted training and performance monitoring for Gurus to
improve customer satisfaction and service consistency.

### 3. Implement Demand-Based Workforce Planning

Align Guru availability and staffing with customer demand patterns.

The analysis identified **08:00 AM as the peak hour**, highlighting
the importance of demand-based workforce planning.
